My Frangle Compendium

ok so hear me out...

The idea of Frangles came to me from the deepest crevaces of my ass my well of divine inspiration. A Frangle is a benign or otherwise unremarkable stimulus (visual, audio, tactile) that evokes an odd emotional response or unearths a memory associated with a strong emotional response. The emotional response elicited by a Frangle is typically complex or undescribable. Likewise, the relationship between the emotional response and the Frangle is unclear, completely dumb, or otherwise difficult to communicate without coming off as strange.

I am infested with the little buggers.

Visual Frangles

  • Wind Socks
    • Every time I look at a wind sock, I feel a muted sense of discomfort and disgust. Initially, I chalked this up to the semi-flacid, not quite conical shape that the concept of windsocks inhabit within this mortal plane.

    While I think that there is some validity in the idea that shapes can cause visceral reactions in some people, my leading theory behind my discomfort is that wind socks visually resemble Pingu. Specifically, they resemble Pingu when he says "Noot Noot". While I did enjoy Pingu as a wee lass, I was terrified of and disturbed by Pingu performing "Noot Noot". The shape of Pingu's beak, the loud, piecing, nasally "Noot", and the unexpectedness of it all was apparently enough to ruin wind socks for me. Pingu was my Vietnam, and "Noot Noot" was the improvised explosive that turned my comrade into hamburger before my eyes.
    Or perhaps I'm just being a tad bit dramatic.
  • Mc Chicken Strips

    A coworker unfortunately informed me of the existence of McChicken Strips. There is no tenderness in my heart for chicky tenders, so I don't gain anything from being aware of this visual abomination, and I ONLY lose out by being burdened with visions of fleshy chasms across sprawling landscapes of chicken meat.
  • The number 83
    • I quite like the number 83. It makes me feel happy and calm, while being visually interesting to look at. It is also the older brother of :3

Tactile Frangles

  • Lines that are too close together
    Whether it be plastic or fabric, any texture that has extruded lines with tight spacing is devil. There is too much uncertainty when touching such materials. Are the lines going to cut me? Why does it feel like I'm simultaneously touching something while also not touching something?
